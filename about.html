Hello! I’m Chunyu Zhang (张淳钰), and this is my personal website.

I am interested in ecology, geography, and the Earth sciences more broadly. I am currently studying theoretical ecology and complex systems science on my own.

Pattern and scale, which connect ecological theory with its applications, remain central issues in ecology. Our understanding of both is strongly constrained by the limits of what we can observe. How are observation, process, and pattern related? How can we describe observations and their consequences? How are scale and observation related? These questions fascinate me.
I conduct independent research, focusing primarily on questions of scale across time, space, and levels of organization in ecological systems. I am also interested in questions of scale in other disciplines, such as the modifiable areal unit problem in geography and renormalization group theory in physics. I hope to deepen my understanding of scale and contribute to the development of theory in this area.
I am currently exploring whether reaction–diffusion equations such as the Fisher–KPP equation can be related to organismal movement in a way that connects different levels of biological organization. The relationship between these equations and the underlying ecological processes remains unclear to me, and I use mathematical modeling to investigate it.

You can find my research materials and updates in the file repository. Some of the files are from earlier work, and I will do my best to keep the repository up to date.

I am currently a third-year undergraduate student majoring in auditing at Nanjing Audit University. As you might have guessed, I don’t particularly enjoy my major.
