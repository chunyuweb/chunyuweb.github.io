I'm Chunyu Zhang. This is my personal website.

I am interested in ecology, geography, and the Earth sciences more broadly. I am currently studying theoretical ecology and complexity science on my own.

I conduct independent research, focusing on temporal, spatial, and organizational scales in ecological systems. I am also interested in questions of scale in other fields, such as the modifiable areal unit problem (MAUP) in geography. My goal is to understand and develop mathematical tools for studying these questions.

Recently, I have been exploring the relationship between reaction–diffusion equations, such as the Fisher–KPP equation, and the movement of organisms. I feel that these equations do not always make the underlying ecological processes explicit, and I am using mathematical modeling to explore this question.

You can follow my research in the file repository. Some files document earlier work, and I will try to keep the repository updated as my work develops.

I am currently a third-year undergraduate studying auditing at Nanjing Audit University, though I do not enjoy my major.
